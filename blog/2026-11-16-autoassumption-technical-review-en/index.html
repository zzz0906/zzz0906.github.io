    AutoAssumption | Searching for ML systems algorithms one assumption at a time

    Review date 2026-11-16 UTC · Author Zhongzhu Zhou
    Paper reviewed AutoAssumption: Searching over Assumptions Discovers ML-Systems Algorithms
    Paper authors Igor Sadalski, Minghao Guo, Benjamin Jones, and Wojciech Matusik
    arXiv 2611.14844v1 · Version date 2026-11-10 UTC

    1. What an assumption buys the search

    When I look at the history of LLM inference, many consequential changes start as a change to something an earlier design took for granted. A target forward pass can verify multiple tentative tokens. Attention keys can be stored in independently managed pages. A pruning mask can allocate zeros across an entire projection matrix. AutoAssumption asks whether an agent can use that sort of choice as the explicit unit of a search. It groups choices extracted from prior designs, edits one choice in a parent, re-derives the affected statements, checks the resulting proof in Lean, and measures the candidate in the parent’s protocol [1, §§1–2].

    The paper’s 89 proof graphs span 44 KV eviction designs, 26 speculative decoding designs, and 19 pruning designs. Appendix F counts composites, alternative settings and standard baselines among those graphs. The optimization experiments start from a smaller set of 26 published parents, split 11, seven and eight across eviction, pruning and decoding respectively. This distinction matters when interpreting coverage. The offline representation covers several dozen designs, while the experiment in which each parent has an improved candidate concerns the selected 26 and their search metrics.

    Figure 1. Source [1, Fig. 1]. Formalization feeds the assumption pool. The candidate’s proof and its benchmark score answer different questions.

    Three descendants make the workflow concrete. RestoreKV GM changes how evidence from query heads sharing a KV head is aggregated. Pruner-Zero LG changes the scope of weight comparisons from each output row to each projection matrix. SVIP LK adds a prompt lookup continuation when the entropy rule stops neural drafting. Group averaging and matrix-wide selection improve the chosen quality scores under the reported protocols. Lookup improves some latency regimes and loses at batch size 32. I treat the variants as experiments with localized interventions in the parent algorithm. The evidence for the search procedure itself comes from the separate comparison to OpenEvolve and AIDE, which is a single eviction search setting.

    2. Prerequisites for reading the proof graphs

    A contract names the property

    Write a contract as a predicate on inputs and outputs. For exact sampling, the output is a probability law over generated sequences. A proof can establish equality to the target’s law for every allowed prefix. For pruning, the output includes a mask of a specified size, so cardinality is an appropriate contract. Perplexity depends on the model and the text distribution, and is measured after pruning. For KV eviction, I would ask whether the method returns a set of in-budget logical entries before asking how much memory a serving system actually allocates. The physical allocator may round, retain masked entries, or require temporary workspace. These are three different observables.

    Lean accepts formal statements and proof terms. Once the formal inputs and semantics are fixed, the checker enforces the derivation. Introducing a probability law as an assumption exposes a dependency the reader can inspect. A benchmark adds information the derivation does not have, such as whether a mean of query-head scores improves retrieval for a particular model. Keeping empirical objectives visible also helps avoid a specification so permissive that any algorithm satisfies it, for example, a cache contract that allows a full cache under every requested ratio.

    Dependency direction and proof repair

    I orient an edge from a premise toward a statement that cites it. For vertices representing assumptions, definitions, lemmas and a final claim, let U(v)U(v)U(v) be immediate users of vvv. Changing aaa requires rechecking the reachable users. Begin with I0={a}I_0=\{a\}I0​={a} and extend the set by one dependency hop at each round.

    It+1=It ∪ ⁣⋃v∈ItU(v),I(a)=⋃t≥0It.(1)I_{t+1}=I_t\ \cup\!\bigcup_{v\in I_t} U(v), \qquad I(a)=\bigcup_{t\ge 0}I_t. \tag{1}It+1​=It​ ∪v∈It​⋃​U(v),I(a)=t≥0⋃​It​.(1)

    The recurrence stops when the set ceases to grow. In a finite directed acyclic graph, a worklist marks each affected vertex once and reads each outgoing edge from a marked vertex once. Then a topological traversal exposes premises before conclusions. In the source paper the document treatment also includes mentions in ranges and summary entries. A semantic dependency can be missing from an informal graph if two definitions silently share a convention. A premise can also acquire a new downstream user after an edit. Both are reasons to re-check the resulting whole contract.

    Figure 2. Source [1, Fig. 3 and App. H]. The fixed-length and acceptance model in the cost branch determines a geometric count. The one-position exactness argument has its own assumptions on sampling and verification.

    Imagine a graph with shared vocabulary, conditional probability laws and fixed block length as starting nodes. The local accept-or-resample lemma consumes the first two. The geometric token-count theorem consumes the third plus a model of prefix acceptance. Replacing fixed length with an adaptive stopping rule preserves a reusable local identity. The full decoder still needs a block-level argument about how the random stop is chosen, which law generated each proposed token, and what token is emitted at the boundary. Tracing dependencies saves effort because it identifies the proof work that remains.

    Two ratios that change the engineering answer

    An eviction ratio such as 32x expresses original logical entries divided by retained logical entries. On a 4,096-token context it corresponds to 128 equivalent positions per layer and KV head on average. A globally ranked mask can place that capacity unevenly. Pruning sparsity is the zeroed fraction of weights in the specified comparison unit. At 50% unstructured sparsity one can choose any half of the matrix. A kernel requiring two zeros in every four contiguous weights has a much smaller admissible set. A quality comparison at equal sparsity therefore answers a different question from a speed comparison at equal hardware constraints.

    3. From a paper collection to a mutation

    The harness first defines a shared problem for a field from a benchmark and a screened set of prior algorithms. A human filters for relevance and reproducibility. A model proposes a contract, and the team tests whether a sample of algorithms can be formalized under it. Appendix A.2 separates this preparation from mutation. In speculative decoding the shared interface identifies the draft and target models and what comes out of a verification step. A contract formulated exclusively around a fixed draft count would impede comparison with adaptive decoders. A contract that says only that the decoder emits tokens would admit arbitrary outcomes.

    The next stage has two agent roles in the paper. One produces a mathematical description with explicit problem, assumptions, definitions and theorems. The other formalizes it in Lean. Across algorithms the authors group assumptions by the design question they answer. In eviction, different algorithms answer where the storage budget is shared, which attention samples inform the score, and how to aggregate head evidence. The resulting catalog can carry a design choice from one family across to another without requiring the entire donor algorithm.

    Algorithm 1. Construct and evaluate one child

    1. Choose a parent design and read its contract and assumption graph.
2. Propose one changed premise and record the predicted effect.
3. Identify direct users and descendants of that premise.
4. Derive new definitions and re-prove the affected claims.
5. When the family has a dial, check its parent-recovering setting.
6. Seek an input that distinguishes the child and screen for duplicates.
7. Require the resulting formal statements to pass Lean.
8. Run the candidate on the parent's benchmark protocol.
9. Record the result and use it to guide the next candidate.

    This is a reading-level summary of §2.2 and Appendices A.2 and D.3. The parent-recovering setting gives a reference within the new family. For a scoring policy, setting λ=0\lambda=0λ=0 could recover a max reducer and setting λ=1\lambda=1λ=1 could select a mean. A useful comparison fixes the replay queries and budget for both. Separating candidates by behavior is stronger than comparing labels. A changed score is still behaviorally redundant in an eviction policy if it induces the same selected mask on every input. A test input where rankings actually swap supplies a witness.

    The approach favors small, attributable changes and lets an agent borrow meaningful alternatives. Whole-program search can uncover interactions in one step, for instance, replacing scoring together with the capacity allocation that depends on it. If two individually inferior edits together improve the design, a greedy chain over one premise can miss the pair. A bounded beam over accepted graphs is a plausible alternative. It retains intermediate designs with distinct tradeoffs at the cost of additional state and evaluator calls. An ablation would have to hold the proposal and evaluation budgets constant to isolate that policy change.

    5. RestoreKV GM. Decide whose evidence counts

    Replay scores and the order of aggregation

    1. Start a normal SVIP draft, bounded by the remaining output budget.

    The lookup can return partial words because it works with token IDs. Appendix C.3 searches the draft-extended prefix for a sufficiently early prior occurrence and available successor span, uses up to ten lookup tokens and sets that cap to zero to recover SVIP. If one knows the next proposal is deterministic given information available before its verification, the per-position draft law is a point mass at ℓ\ellℓ. The acceptance probability for ℓ\ellℓ is p(ℓ)p(\ell)p(ℓ) and the rejection branch redistributes the remaining mass over the other tokens. Thus deterministic lookup is inside the algebra of (2)–(4) under correct conditioning. Pooled lookup behavior that depends on future verifier outcomes would need a new argument. Appendix H’s worked theorem concerns a fixed-length idealized loop and explicitly excludes arbitrary stopping rules and the measured cap branch.

    Appendix I.2 constructs 14 single-bug mutations across five eviction parents and five controls, using a fixed parent specification.     9. Limitations and conditions for interpreting the experiments

    The paper names human specification review, fields with empirical success criteria, and noncanonical assumption decompositions as limitations [1, §6]. Each is consequential for the result. The contract starts from algorithms that run on the available benchmark. Some published designs will need a broader interface or a different evaluator to be represented fairly. The underlying automated discovery cost includes screening prior work, moving designs into a common protocol and formalizing each parent. Repeated use can amortize part of that investment. The one-parent search comparison leaves its magnitude unmeasured. For a single isolated optimization I would count that cost before crediting the same model-call budget.

    Appendix G also describes different uncertainty units. Eviction primarily resamples questions within tasks, reported over 10,000 bootstrap draws, and some cross-cell summaries use independent-cell aggregation. Reusing the same questions can correlate cell effects. The pruning bootstrap samples WikiText-2 windows within one nominal calibration run, so it leaves out different calibration samples, gradient draws and model checkpoints. Spec-Bench’s three repeated runs at a fixed seed measure timing variation, while the batch-profile interval comes from paired question resampling. A narrow interval for one source of randomness leaves uncertainty over the others.

    There is a broader distinction between measuring a descendant and attributing improvement to the harness. Section 4 chooses the best candidate per parent after several trials. Appendix Table 13 shows that 16 of RestoreKV’s 36 variants beat its measured search score, while Pruner-Zero has two improvements among ten variants. A best-of-nnn statistic is likely to improve even when many single candidates do not. If noisy candidate scores share mean μ\muμ and have independent symmetric errors, taking a maximum gives positive expected optimism for n>1n>1n>1 whenever those errors vary. The distributions are neither independent nor identical here, but the selection effect still motivates a fresh score for frozen winners. The full RULER cohort includes the search subset. The longer RULER settings and LongBench shifts give complementary evidence.

    10. Critical analysis

    Make the hard contract measurable at the service boundary

    The most persuasive demonstration in Appendix I pairs every candidate with a fixed invariant. The central question for real serving is whether the invariant is the one the owner cares about. A formal bound on live KV pairs with a mask describes the mathematical cache, while an allocator consumes bytes in pages and can temporarily allocate scoring state. I would state two separate constraints before a run. The first is a maximum logical retained count, with sinks and restore pairs charged to it. The second is a measured peak bytes and TTFT budget in a named serving regime. Apply both to baseline and child and log rejected variants. A memory-overrun mutant then gets a clear reason for exclusion whether or not Lean was used. The remaining value of the proof is that a universal property covers input classes the finite evaluator may never generate.

    A counterexample clarifies what belongs in the specification. Consider two proof-equivalent masks that retain exactly 128 entries on average. One packs the surviving entries contiguously. The other retains one entry from each of 128 different 16-entry pages. With a page-granular allocator and no compaction, the latter can hold 128×16128\times16128×16 slots for the 128 survivors. The proof of logical cardinality applies to both. The observed memory can differ by up to 16x in this example. A budget guarantee on physical pages must include the mapping, and a practical compaction step introduces migration cost. This matters particularly at the aggressive ratios where the paper sees the largest quality gain.

    Factor the search comparison before extending it to other domains

    The reported comparison changes the proposal representation, available prior information and search strategy together. I would first build a two-by-two test of one-premise moves and proof gating. Let all arms see the same parent artifacts and assumption catalog, provide the same mandatory small-model contract tests, and fix a budget that includes failed proof repairs and proposals that never reach the GPU. Then vary whether the agent must express one assumption change and whether it must deliver a proof. Random draws from the same hand-written assumption alternatives are a useful baseline. The contrast between them and model-guided selection isolates whether the model is needed for ranking edits. Adding a separate beam experiment tests whether a localized mutation benefit depends on greedy acceptance.

    The current paper motivates that experiment but cannot substitute for it. Its code-level baselines make an early jump, suggesting the space they can traverse has high-return multi-edit moves. If a problem has interactions between grouping and budgeting, any single edit may be counterproductive until the second follows. Measure the frequency of such interactions by choosing pairs of already screened amendments, comparing each edit alone and then jointly. Capture the score and practical cost at each point. On a common score scale the interaction contrast is

    J=f(a,b)−f(a,0)−f(0,b)+f(0,0).(13)J=f(a,b)-f(a,0)-f(0,b)+f(0,0). \tag{13}J=f(a,b)−f(a,0)−f(0,b)+f(0,0).(13)

    This follows by subtracting the change caused by bbb in the untouched parent from the change caused by bbb after aaa is in place. Positive JJJ means positive interaction on that scale. It does not ensure that either edit is individually attractive. For a score rising from 70 at the parent to 69 and 68 for singles and 75 jointly, J=8J=8J=8 points and strict hill climbing rejects both first moves. A small factorial test on a locked validation set could quantify how often the one-premise policy would miss a pair that matters.

    Test the stability of the assumption representation

    Two formalizers can split the same design differently. One defines replay score as a single expression. Another writes separate assumptions about visible keys, reduction axes and budget. Their Lean proofs can conclude the same contract, while the second induces many more one-step neighbors. To test stability I would ask independent formalizers to start from the same parent and contract, align their premise classes by behavioral probes, then report downstream-set size and overlap of generated variants. If the same resource budget repeatedly produces different quality winners across representations, the catalog is partly a prior supplied by the formalizer. Interpreting it that way allows comparisons to stronger manually designed parameter spaces.

    A concise stress test would pit specialist retrieval against diffuse evidence for RestoreKV GM. Use long contexts with two matched classes. In one, the answer depends on an entry scored highly by just one query head. In the other, several heads attend to the relevant entry. Pre-register the subset choice and report the mask’s retention probability before scoring text outputs. Continuous mixtures of max and mean, with the parent-recovery endpoint held fixed, make it possible to test whether the chosen aggregate tracks the cause of failure. The paper provides a plausible ranking mechanism. It does not identify which heads were responsible for the actual RULER wins.

    Require a distinct locked estimate after selection

    The search uses a small question cohort. Comparing best-of-run curves under that cohort measures progress on the evaluator, and multiple graph decompositions encourage trying many locally valid variants. I would retain the 200-question set for proposals and reserve a disjoint question set for deciding which design to advance. A third locked set should be used once, after choosing a finalist. Report the frontier over accuracy, costs and memory on that locked set. When two methods tie under mild compression, show high-pressure cases at equal bytes. For SVIP LK, separate pure text with few repeated phrases from repetition-heavy workloads and include a batch-adaptive no-lookup policy. These are proposed tests, not outcomes.

    11. Reading the experiments independently

    Appendices A and C contain enough context to reconstruct the measurement questions even before any systems run. For RULER, collect the 12 equally weighted cells, preserve the question partition, and report both the average and the factor-specific profile. For pruning, record gradient-text and held-out text splits, the dense reference and the exact mask cardinality per projection. For speculative decoding, separate greedy sequence agreement, target-law exactness under stated assumptions, accepted tokens and wall-clock time, including GPU synchronization. Appendix G specifies which quantity the reported intervals estimate. A useful report includes both the initial screening setup and the larger or shifted evaluation for each result.

    In this review I re-derived the overlap and residual identities, checked the grouping and cardinality examples, and recomputed ratios from the paper’s tables. Those hand calculations and the redrawn figures use the cited manuscript. They do not represent another run of a model, a GPU benchmark or a Lean proof.

    12. Conclusion

    AutoAssumption gives a practical representation for comparing the design choices made by ML systems algorithms. A changed premise identifies which definitions and theorems require attention and produces a concrete parent-child comparison on a common benchmark. The locality is especially clear for query-head reduction and projection-wide pruning. The exact-sampling example makes the boundary visible at the level of a proof: distributional correctness follows from the proposal and verification law, while speed depends on survival, batch shape and resource costs. At the reported search budget, gains from this representation accumulate late. Measuring independent runs across more contracts and accounting for preparation costs would establish when that investment pays off.
