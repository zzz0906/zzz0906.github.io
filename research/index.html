    Research

    Research portfolio

    My research spans efficient machine learning and systems — model architecture, post-training and reinforcement learning, quantization, ML engines, systems, and infrastructure, and agentic AI. For paper details and authors, see Selected Publications or my Google Scholar profile.

    Efficient ML Architecture

    Architecture co-design for speculative decoding and attention, so large models run with less communication stall, lower KV cost, and better test-time reasoning efficiency.

    Efficient Speculator Design

    • Aurora ICML 2026

      Unified training–serving loop that continuously learns a speculator from live inference traces for day-0 deployment and adaptive speedups.

    • Osprey

      Speculator architecture exploration for higher acceptance and lower draft overhead in production serving.

    • MixOfSpeculator

      Mix-architecture draft models that combine complementary speculative pathways for more stable acceptance.

    • Phoenix Speculator

      Speculative decoding design with cache-aware prompting to stabilize acceptance under realistic serving traffic.

    • VocabPrune

      Vocabulary pruning for smaller, faster draft models without collapsing target-model coverage.

    • Diffusion Router

      Diffusion-style MoE routing for smoother expert selection than hard top-k dispatch.

    Efficient Attention Design

    • Imitate Optimal Policy Under review

      Studies action collapse in policy gradients and how imitating optimal policies can stabilize large-action-space RL training.

    • CorDA NeurIPS 2024

      Context-oriented covariance decomposition for task-aware PEFT that preserves pretrained knowledge while adapting to new tasks.

    • Ladder-Residual ICML 2025

      Parallelism-aware residual path that overlaps communication with computation under tensor parallelism for large-model inference.

    • CREST Under review

      Training-free test-time steering of cognitive attention heads — higher reasoning accuracy with fewer generated tokens.

    • CARE ICLR 2026

      Converts pretrained GQA/MHA checkpoints into MLA-compatible attention with covariance-aware, rank-enhanced decomposition.

    • I-DLM COLM 2026

      Introspective diffusion language models that verify prior tokens while generating new ones in a single forward pass.

    • Taylor-Calibrate Under review

      Taylor-guided initialization for hybrid linear-attention distillation, cutting tokens needed to recover teacher quality.

    • Loop Diffusion

      Looping diffusion formulations for iterative refinement during generation.

    Efficient Post-Training, RL & Quantization

    Loss design, RL objectives, and low-bit KV / weight compression that keep training and serving quality while shrinking compute and memory.

    Efficient Loss Design

    • Scaling Law of Speculative Decoding

      Characterizes how speculative decoding speedups scale with draft quality, acceptance, and system constraints.

    • Tail Likelihood Reinforcement Learning

      RL objective design that emphasizes difficult / long-tail cases instead of average-case reward only.

    • Mini-Batch Scaling Laws in Sketched Linear Regression arXiv 2026

      From one-pass SGD to data reuse: scaling laws for mini-batch sketched linear regression.

    • Sketched Linear Contrastive Learning arXiv 2026

      Approximation, optimization, and statistical scaling for sketched linear contrastive learning.

    Quantization

    • Flash-LLM VLDB 2024

      Load-as-sparse / compute-as-dense SpMM on GPU Tensor Cores for unstructured-sparse LLM inference.

    • Quant-LLM USENIX ATC 2024

      FP6-centric algorithm–system co-design that brings Tensor-Core support to unconventional bit-widths.

    • KITTY MLSys 2026

      2-bit KV cache with dynamic channel-wise precision boost for accuracy–throughput tradeoffs at large batch.

    • SAW-INT4 Under review

      System-aware 4-bit KV-cache quantization tailored to real-world LLM serving stacks.

    • OSCAR ACL 2026 · Under review

      Offline spectral covariance-aware rotations for accurate 2-bit KV cache quantization at serving scale.
